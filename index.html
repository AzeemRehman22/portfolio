Information About me

I’m a Senior Mobile Developer with 4+ years of experience building secure, scalable, and high-performance mobile applications, primarily for Android using Kotlin and Java. My expertise includes Clean Architecture, MVVM, offline-first design, and performance optimization. I also have hands-on experience with Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP) for cross-platform architecture, and I design and ship AI-driven mobile experiences — building autonomous AI agents with LangChain and LangGraph, integrating LLMs and RAG pipelines into production apps, and writing Python scripts for automation, data processing, and workflow orchestration. I’m known for writing clean, maintainable code with a strong focus on user experience, scalability, and thoughtful AI integration. Outside of work, I enjoy traveling and exploring new technologies — especially the fast-moving world of AI agents and tooling — which keeps me inspired and continuously learning.
